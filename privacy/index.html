Privacy Policy

Last updated November 16, 2026

What stays on your device

Your results, streak, stats, level, badges, saved games and settings are stored only on your device. Clutch Call has no accounts and no servers of its own. We don't collect your name, email, contacts or location, and we never sell your information. Deleting the app deletes this data.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

Clutch Call is free and shows ads served by Google AdMob: banner ads on some menu and results screens, occasional full-screen ads between practice games, and optional videos you can choose to watch for an in-game reward. Google may collect and use:

  • your device's advertising identifier (IDFA), only if you allow tracking when iOS asks
  • your IP address, which can be used to estimate a general location
  • information about the ads you see and tap, plus basic device and app information

Google uses this to show, measure and limit ads, to personalize ads where you allow it, and to prevent fraud. You can change tracking anytime in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking. Players in regions that require it (such as the EEA, UK and Switzerland) are asked for consent first and can change their choice in the app under Settings → Privacy & ad choices.

How Google uses data: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

Notifications and sharing

The daily reminder is an optional local notification scheduled on your device; no push server is involved. When you share a result, your phone's share sheet sends the text you choose. We don't receive it.

Children

Clutch Call is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.
