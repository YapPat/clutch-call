Support

Questions, bugs or ideas? Email yp7buenapark@gmail.com and we'll get back to you.

How do I play?

Each possession, pick a play (or a defense) and a tempo, then call it. Read the defense's look before you call: hold any play card to see it run. Settings → How to play replays the intro.

When does the new game come out?

Every day at midnight UTC. Everyone in the world plays the same game that day.

I closed the app mid-game.

Your daily is saved after every call. Open the app and tap Resume.

How does the coach's grade work?

Each call is graded on what you could see when you made it (the defense's look, the score, the clock), not on whether the shot went in.

How do I turn off sounds, haptics or the reminder?

Open Settings from the gear on the Home screen.

Does it need internet?

No. The game plays offline. Ads need a connection.
